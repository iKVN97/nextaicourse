02What this website collects

This website sets no cookies and loads no third-party resources by default. The one exception is Meta Pixel, described immediately below, which only loads if you explicitly accept it.

The homepage uses your browser's session storage for one purpose only: remembering, for the current browser tab, that you have already seen the opening animation, so it does not replay if you scroll back to the top. This is never sent to a server, is not used to identify or track you, and disappears when you close the tab. It is not a cookie and does not require consent under applicable law.

Meta Pixel — advertising measurement and conversion tracking. We use Meta Pixel (operated by Meta Platforms, Inc.) to measure which ads lead to a course purchase. It is not active by default: on your first visit, a banner asks you to accept or decline advertising cookies. Meta Pixel only loads, and only then starts sending data to Meta, if you accept. If you decline, it never loads. Either way, your choice is remembered in your browser's local storage so you are not asked again on later visits; this stored choice is not a cookie itself and is never sent to a server. We do not use TikTok Pixel, Google Analytics, or any other analytics or advertising tracker.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. As with any web host, GitHub's servers automatically record technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the time of your request, in order to serve the page and protect the service. We do not control those server logs and do not use them to identify you.

The "Get access" button on this site calls our own payment backend, hosted on Cloudflare Workers (Cloudflare, Inc.), to start a Stripe Checkout session. This is not an analytics, advertising or tracking resource — it exists solely to create your purchase and cannot be used to identify you before you choose to pay.

03Information collected when you buy

Payments are processed by Stripe. When you make a purchase, Stripe collects the information needed to take payment — typically your name, email address, payment details and billing country. We never receive or store your full card details.

We receive transaction information from Stripe, such as your name, email address, the amount paid, the date and your country. We use it to give you access to the course, provide support, handle refunds and meet our tax obligations.

04Course access and access email

After a successful payment, our backend (hosted on Cloudflare Workers, using Cloudflare's KV storage to record your purchase, email address, and an access token identifier) sends you an access email through Resend, our transactional email provider. Resend processes your email address and the delivery status of that one message on our behalf — it does not receive your payment details.

The link in that email contains a signed access token used only to open the course — it does not identify you to any third party and cannot be used to make a purchase. Opening the course app stores that token in your browser's session storage (not a cookie) so you don't have to reopen the email every visit; it clears when you close the tab and is never sent anywhere except our own course backend to load your lesson content.

If you contact us to cancel/withdraw (see our Refund Policy), we record that request — your email address, the purchase it relates to, and the date — to process your refund and meet our recordkeeping obligations.

Before payment, when you tick the immediate-delivery consent box at checkout, we record that a consent was given, which exact wording and version you saw, and the timestamp — linked to your purchase once payment completes. We keep this because the law requires us to be able to show what you agreed to; we do not use it for anything beyond that and beyond ordinary support/tax recordkeeping.

05Why we process your data, and on what legal basis

  • To perform our contract with you — giving you access to the course, handling refunds and answering support requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations — in particular retaining transaction and VAT records under Dutch tax law.
  • Our legitimate interests — keeping the site secure and preventing fraud and abuse.
  • Your consent — only if and when we introduce tracking that legally requires it.

06Who we share it with

We share personal data only where necessary, with Stripe (payment processing) and GitHub (website hosting). We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for advertising purposes.

07International transfers

Stripe and GitHub may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where that happens, transfers are made under safeguards permitted by the GDPR, such as a European Commission adequacy decision or Standard Contractual Clauses.

08How long we keep it

Transaction and invoice records are kept for seven years, as required by Dutch tax law. Other personal data is kept only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for.

09Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@nextaicourse.com. You can also reach us by telephone using the details in section 10. We will respond within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch supervisory authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).
