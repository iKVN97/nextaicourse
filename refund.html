In short

NEXT AI COURSE is delivered as immediate digital access. There is no free trial and no voluntary "money-back guarantee" — it is not designed to be taken for a couple of weeks and handed back. 01No trial, no change-of-mind refund

VK Sales does not offer a satisfaction guarantee, a trial period, or a "use it and send it back" policy for this course. The entire course — every module, lesson and prompt — is available to you within seconds of payment, so there is nothing held back to return. This is not "no refunds under any circumstances". Section 4 lists the situations we always put right, on their own merits, with no deadline tied to your purchase date.

02Immediate access and the right of withdrawal

A distance purchase like this one would normally carry a statutory 14-day right of withdrawal — the right to cancel within 14 days without giving a reason. For digital content that is supplied immediately rather than on a disc or other tangible medium, the law (Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU Art. 16(m), implemented in the Netherlands in Book 6 of the Burgerlijk Wetboek) lets that right end as soon as supply begins, provided the consumer has first, before supply starts:

  • expressly requested that supply begin immediately, during the 14-day period; and
  • expressly acknowledged that doing so means the right of withdrawal is lost once supply has begun.

Our checkout collects exactly those two things, in one plain-language statement you must actively tick — it is never pre-selected, and you cannot pay without it. When you tick it and complete payment, your access begins immediately and your 14-day change-of-mind right of withdrawal ends at that point. We record the exact wording and version you agreed to and repeat it in your access email; that email, with this page, is your confirmation on a durable medium.

If a particular purchase was made without that consent being validly recorded — for example a technical fault on our side — then the statutory 14-day right of withdrawal was never given up and stays fully intact for that purchase: tell us within 14 days using the form below and we refund the €39.99 in full, no reason needed. We do not decide this against you; we check the record and tell you honestly which case applies.

04Situations we always put right

The absence of a change-of-mind refund does not touch the following. These are not favours and not discretionary; they apply on their own merits, with no deadline tied to your purchase date, and nothing elsewhere on this page limits them:

  • Duplicate charge — you were charged more than once for the same purchase. We refund the extra charge(s).
  • Incorrect amount charged — you were charged something other than the advertised €39.99. We refund the difference, or the full amount if the charge was not one you authorised.
  • Access never supplied — your payment succeeded but you were never given a working way into the course, and we cannot put that right. Full refund.
  • Defective or non-conforming digital content — the course is materially broken, inaccessible through a fault on our side, or does not match what was described at the time of purchase. Under Dutch and EU consumer law (the rules on conformity of digital content, Art. 7:50 ff. of the Burgerlijk Wetboek implementing Directive (EU) 2019/770) you are entitled to have it brought into conformity and, where that is not done within a reasonable time or fails, to a price reduction or a refund. We would rather fix it — tell us what is wrong and we will.

Email support@nextaicourse.com for any of these, or use the form in section 3. Nothing on this page removes or reduces any other mandatory remedy you have under Dutch or EU consumer law.

05How a refund is paid, when one is due

A refund is due where the statutory withdrawal right was retained for your purchase (section 2, last paragraph) or where one of the situations in section 4 applies and cannot be resolved another way. When one is due, we make it to your original payment method through Stripe within 14 days of being informed, usually much sooner. Your bank or card issuer may take a few more days to show it. You are never charged a fee for a valid refund.

06This policy cannot reduce your mandatory rights

Everything above describes how we handle voluntary refunds and how the withdrawal right interacts with immediate digital delivery. It is not an attempt to contract out of consumer protection. Any right the law gives you that cannot be waived — including the conformity remedies in section 4 and, where it was retained, the right of withdrawal in section 2 — applies in full whatever this page says.
