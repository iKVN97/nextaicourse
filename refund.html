01Our voluntary 14-day refund

VK Sales offers a voluntary refund period of 14 days from the date of purchase. Within that period you can request a full refund of the €39.99 you paid, without giving a reason.

02Your statutory right of withdrawal

Separately from our voluntary policy, EU and Dutch consumer law gives you a statutory 14-day right of withdrawal on distance purchases.

For digital content, that right can be lost only if you expressly consent to immediate delivery and acknowledge losing the right. We do not ask you to do either, so your statutory right of withdrawal remains intact.

Our voluntary policy sits alongside your statutory rights. It does not replace or limit them.
