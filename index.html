01THE GAP

You use AI. But did anyone ever teach you how?

Most people learned AI by typing a question into a box and hoping. That works for trivia. It falls apart the moment the task actually matters — because the skill was never the typing. It's everything around it.

How most people ask

Result — generic, rewritten three times, still not usable
How you'll ask

context: B2B tool, 200 users, €0 ad budget task: 90-day acquisition plan limits: 2 channels max, no paid ads format: table + assumptions + risks check: flag anything you're unsure about

01

Context

You know the situation, the constraints and what "good" looks like. The model knows none of it until you say so.

02

Structure

There's a difference between asking a question and writing a brief. One gets you an answer, the other gets you work.

03

Verification

AI sounds equally confident when it's right and when it's wrong. Knowing how to check is part of the skill.

04

Repetition

Most people rebuild the same task from scratch every single time, instead of building it once and reusing it.

The Method.
